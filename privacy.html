Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BetetaAI collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when individuals contact us, request our services, or choose to receive SMS communications from BetetaAI.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, business or service inquiry details, appointment information, SMS message content, message delivery information, timestamps, consent records, and opt-out preferences.

We collect this information when you submit a contact or SMS opt-in form, contact us directly, or communicate with us about our services.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, discuss and provide requested services, confirm or update appointments, send service-related follow-ups, maintain communication records, operate our automation systems, and protect our systems against misuse.

Our SMS program covers customer inquiry acknowledgments, appointment confirmations or updates, and service-related follow-up messages. Message frequency varies based on your interactions with BetetaAI. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS consent and your choices

We send automated SMS communications under this program only to recipients who have provided the appropriate consent. Where we collect consent through an online form, the form identifies BetetaAI, describes the messages, and requires an affirmative opt-in. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

You may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time or HELP for assistance. You may also contact us at the email address below to ask questions about your information or request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.

Information sharing and SMS consent

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers, such as communications, hosting, and workflow infrastructure providers (including Twilio), to process information and deliver messages on our behalf. They may access information only as needed to provide those services and are not authorized by BetetaAI to use it for independent marketing.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain communication and consent records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our systems. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to safeguard information and remove or de-identify records when they are no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or SMS support, contact BetetaAI at luis@betetaai.com.
