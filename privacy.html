Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BetetaAI handles personal information associated with its internal automated SMS development and testing campaign.

Information we collect

For testing and troubleshooting, we may process the approved recipient's mobile telephone number, test message content, simulated inquiry details, SMS delivery status, timestamps, records of consent, and opt-out preferences. We do not invite the general public to subscribe to this internal test campaign.

How information is used

We use this information solely to develop, test, troubleshoot, secure, and validate automated communications and related workflow integrations. Message frequency varies with testing activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing and SMS consent

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers, including Twilio, to process or deliver messages on our behalf, subject to appropriate operational and security requirements. These providers are not authorized to use our test recipients' data for independent marketing.

Retention and security

We retain test records only as needed for development, troubleshooting, security, compliance, or applicable legal obligations, and take reasonable steps to protect them. We remove records that are no longer necessary.

Your choices

Participants may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance. The currently authorized recipient may request deletion of test information, subject to any applicable retention obligations.
