You may opt in by submitting BetetaAI's SMS consent form and affirmatively selecting the SMS consent checkbox. By opting in, you authorize BetetaAI to send the described messages to the mobile number you provide. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Opting out

You can cancel SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to a message from BetetaAI. After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message. You will not receive further messages under this program unless you opt in again.
